Methodology

Last reviewed: August 27, 2026.

Paycheck calculation order

Gross annual wages → pre-tax deductions → federal taxable income and annual federal tax liability → FICA → state taxable income and state income tax → supported state payroll deductions → supported local tax → post-tax deductions → net pay. The displayed paycheck federal-tax line is the annual federal tax liability prorated by the selected pay period; it is not an IRS W-4 withholding calculation.

Federal income tax

The engine uses 2026 federal marginal rates and standard deductions. For 2026 the standard deduction is $16,100 single/MFS, $32,200 MFJ, and $24,150 HOH. See the official IRS 2026 inflation adjustment and Revenue Procedure 2025-32 for the rate tables.

FICA

Social Security uses 6.2% up to the 2026 $184,500 wage base. Medicare uses 1.45% with no wage cap. Additional Medicare is calculated at 0.9% above the filing-status threshold; payroll withholding uses the federal $200,000 employer trigger.

State income tax

State tax uses the state data layer. Progressive states use published bracket tables where verified; flat-tax states use their published flat rate; no-income-tax states return $0 state individual income tax. Where a filing status lacks a verified bracket model, the engine does not silently reuse Single—it returns a warning and skips state income tax for that status.

State payroll taxes

Supported employee deductions are calculated separately and shown as their own ledger rows. California SDI is 1.3% of all wages in 2026. Washington PFML uses the 2026 1.13% total premium and 71.43% employee share, subject to the $184,500 Social Security wage base; WA Cares uses 0.58% with no wage cap. Other state payroll deductions may be unmodeled.

Local taxes

Only jurisdictions in data/local-tax-rules-2026.js are modeled: New York City, Philadelphia, and Detroit. NYC is resident-only; Philadelphia and Detroit use resident/nonresident rates. When no local jurisdiction is selected, local tax is not invented or guessed, and states known to levy local tax are flagged.

Deductions

Traditional 401(k), HSA, and health-premium inputs are treated according to the state/federal treatment represented in the deduction layer. Pre-tax and post-tax deductions are separate, and the paycheck ledger displays pre-tax deductions so its arithmetic reconciles to net pay.

What remains unmodeled

  • Many city/county/occupational taxes outside NYC, Philadelphia, and Detroit.
  • ZIP/address-level local tax lookup.
  • Many state-specific credits, itemized deductions, and special filing rules.
  • Employer-specific benefit and withholding elections.
Important: a modeled rule is still an estimate, not a payroll guarantee. See the Disclaimer for limitations.

Sources

Federal: IRS 2026 inflation adjustments. Social Security: SSA 2026 wage base. California SDI: California EDD. Washington PFML: Washington Paid Leave. New York City: New York Tax Department.
