California Paycheck Calculator 2026 — How Much Is $80K a Year After Taxes?

You earn $80,000 a year in California. Your gross salary is $6,666.67 per month, but your bank deposit is lower because federal income tax, California income tax, FICA, and California SDI are separate layers.

Current calculator estimate: for a single employee earning $80,000 with no voluntary pre-tax deductions, US Salary Tools estimates about $60,722.02 annual take-home, or about $5,060.17 per month. This is an estimate for planning, not a guaranteed paycheck result.

Why the number is lower than $80,000

The calculator applies the 2026 federal standard deduction and marginal tax brackets, California's progressive income-tax schedule, Social Security, Medicare, and California SDI. California SDI is separate from California personal income tax.

California uses progressive tax brackets

California does not tax the entire salary at one flat percentage. Different portions of taxable income move through different rates. For 2026 estimates, the engine uses the latest published California rate schedules available to it and applies the correct filing-status schedule for the modeled status.

California SDI in 2026

The 2026 employee SDI rate used by the calculator is 1.3% with no wage cap in the current data model, so an $80,000 wage produces about $1,040 of SDI.

What changes the result?

Your filing status, 401(k), HSA, health premiums, withholding choices, local taxes, credits, and employer payroll setup can all change the actual number. The calculator is intentionally an estimate rather than a payroll-system replica.
