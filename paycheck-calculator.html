How this estimate works

Federal income tax

2026 federal tax brackets and standard deduction are modeled. W-4 withholding is kept distinct from annual tax liability.

FICA and state payroll taxes

Social Security, Medicare, and supported state employee payroll deductions are calculated separately and shown in the result.

State income tax

Where state bracket data is modeled, progressive brackets are used instead of a single flat approximation.

Local taxes

Supported local jurisdictions can be selected and modeled. Other city/county taxes remain explicitly unmodeled rather than guessed.

Bottom line: this is an estimate for informational purposes only. Exact payroll results depend on employer setup, local rules, credits, benefits, and individual circumstances. Full methodology: Methodology.
