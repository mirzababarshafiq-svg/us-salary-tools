How this estimate works

Full transparency on every assumption baked into this calculator:

Federal income tax

2026 IRS marginal brackets (10% – 37%) applied to your income after the 2026 standard deduction ($16,100 single / $32,200 married filing jointly / $24,150 head of household). No credits, dependents, or itemized deductions.

FICA (Social Security & Medicare)

6.2% Social Security up to the 2026 wage base of $184,500, plus 1.45% Medicare on all wages, plus 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax above $200,000 ($250,000 married filing jointly; $125,000 married filing separately).

State income tax

A simplified flat-rate approximation per state — not your state's real bracket structure, standard deduction, or local/city taxes. The 9 states with no wage income tax show $0.

What's not included

Pre-tax deductions (401(k), HSA, health insurance), tax credits (Child Tax Credit, EITC), local/city income taxes, and self-employment tax.

Bottom line: this is an estimate for informational purposes only. It is not tax advice and does not guarantee accuracy. Consult a licensed tax professional or your employer's payroll department for exact figures. Full methodology on our Methodology page.
