Disclaimer

Last updated: August 27, 2026

In short: every result on this site is an estimate for informational purposes. It is not tax, legal, accounting, or financial advice, and US Salary Tools is not affiliated with any government agency.

General disclaimer

US Salary Tools provides simplified 2026 salary, paycheck, overtime, raise, and state-tax estimates. Results can differ from an actual paycheck or tax return because payroll setup, credits, deductions, benefits, employer elections, location-specific rules, and other facts can change the outcome.

Paycheck Calculator specific disclaimer

The Paycheck Calculator uses 2026 federal tax liability, FICA, modeled state income-tax rules, supported state payroll deductions, and selected local taxes. State systems may be progressive, flat, or have no individual income tax. Traditional 401(k), HSA, and health-premium inputs are supported. Local income tax is modeled only for supported jurisdictions such as New York City, Philadelphia, and Detroit when selected; other city, county, and municipal taxes remain estimates or unmodeled.

The displayed federal paycheck line is an annual federal tax liability prorated by pay period; it is not a claim that an employer's W-4 withholding will exactly match IRS payroll withholding tables.

State payroll taxes

Supported employee payroll deductions such as California SDI, New York Paid Family Leave, and Washington PFML/WA Cares are shown separately where modeled. Unsupported or employer-specific payroll deductions may be missing.

No government affiliation

US Salary Tools is independently operated and is not affiliated with the IRS, SSA, U.S. Department of Labor, or any state or local government.

Accuracy & changes

Tax rules change over time. We use published 2026 rules where available, but some state/local rules remain modeled or unverified. Review the Methodology page and official tax agencies for important decisions.
